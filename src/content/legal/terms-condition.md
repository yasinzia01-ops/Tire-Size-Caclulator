## Calculator Results Are Estimates

All results are theoretical values calculated from the numbers on a tire’s sidewall and a wheel’s specification (section width, aspect ratio, rim diameter, rim width and offset). Real-world dimensions can differ because of:

- differences between tire brands and models of the same nominal size;
- tire pressure, load, wear and the width of the rim the tire is mounted on;
- suspension setup, ride height, camber and manufacturing tolerances of your vehicle.

Results for diameter, circumference, poke, inset, ride height, speedometer error, revolutions per mile and ideal rim width are provided for general guidance only. Always confirm fitment, clearance and load/speed ratings with the tire or wheel manufacturer, your vehicle manufacturer, or a qualified tire professional before buying or fitting wheels and tires.
