This Privacy Policy explains what information TireSizeCalculator.pro (“the site”, “we”, “us”) collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. The site is a free set of tire and wheel calculators and guides. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.

## Information We Collect

**Calculator inputs.** Every calculator on this site (tire size, speedometer error, revolutions per mile and wheel offset) runs entirely in your browser. The tire sizes, rim widths and offsets you enter are not sent to us or stored by us.

**Analytics data.** We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, the website that referred you, your approximate location (country/city, derived from your IP address), and your device, browser and operating system. This information is aggregated and does not tell us who you are.

**Server logs.** Like almost every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when your browser requests a page — for example IP address, date and time, the page requested and browser type. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running reliably.

## Cookies

We do not use cookies for advertising, and the site does not show ads.

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`) to tell new visits from returning ones and to measure usage over time. These cookies do not contain your name or contact details.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and you can opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The calculators work normally with cookies disabled.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

- operate the site, keep it secure and fix problems;
- understand which pages and calculators are useful so we can improve them;
- measure overall traffic and trends.

We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not build advertising profiles.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Analytics** (Google LLC) processes analytics data on our behalf. Learn how Google uses this data at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Hosting.** The site is served by a web hosting provider that processes server logs as described above.

Our pages link to other websites, such as NHTSA, SAE International and FuelEconomy.gov. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies when you visit them.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above so they can provide their services to us, or where required by law.

## Data Retention

Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in our Google Analytics account and is then deleted automatically. Server logs are kept only for as long as the hosting provider needs them for security and operations.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not collect names, emails or accounts, we generally cannot link analytics data to an individual. The simplest way to control your data on this site is to block cookies or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on described above.

## Children’s Privacy

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We serve the site over HTTPS and rely on reputable providers. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
